# Feature Documentation - Automation and AI

Pipeline Automation: rules for stale-deal alerts, follow-up tasks and discount approval routing. Conversation Intelligence: records and transcribes calls, detects objections and competitor mentions. AI Revenue Copilot: natural-language questions over pipeline and revenue data, billed by usage; answers cite the source records.
