# Nexora Conversation Intelligence - Product Description

**Nexora Conversation Intelligence** (AI & Insights). Call recording, transcription and objection detection.

Pricing: Per User, list $35.00 per unit per month, billed monthly (product_id 9).

Key capabilities:
- Competitor mention detection
- Talk-time analytics
- Deal risk flags

Limitation: English, German and French only.
