# Nexora Revenue Analytics - Product Description

**Nexora Revenue Analytics** (Analytics). Dashboards and cohort analysis across pipeline and revenue.

Pricing: Tiered, list $1,500.00 per unit per month, billed quarterly (product_id 2).

Key capabilities:
- Win rate, discount and cycle-time dashboards
- MRR/ARR movement
- Scheduled exports

Limitation: Slow above 1 million rows (NX-4412).
