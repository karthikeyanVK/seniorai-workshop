# Nexora Customer Health Monitor - Product Description

**Nexora Customer Health Monitor** (Customer Success). Account health scoring and trend monitoring.

Pricing: Per Account, list $6.00 per unit per month, billed quarterly (product_id 7).

Key capabilities:
- Usage, adoption and ticket signals
- Churn probability
- At-risk alerts

Limitation: Needs a product usage feed from the customer.
