# Pricing and Packaging Guide

Plans: **Starter** (up to 25 users, standard support, no SSO), **Professional** (26-250 users, SAML SSO, API access, standard integrations), **Enterprise** (251+ users, SSO, advanced audit logs, EU data residency, premium SLA). Sales Cloud lists at $95/user/month billed annually. Modules are priced separately: Revenue Analytics $1,500/mo, Forecast Pro $900/mo, Pipeline Automation $1,200/mo, Data Connect $1,100/mo, Security & Compliance Pack $2,000/mo, Premier Support $1,500/mo. In the structured data, subscription plan_name follows ACV: Starter < $25k, Professional < $100k, Enterprise >= $100k.
