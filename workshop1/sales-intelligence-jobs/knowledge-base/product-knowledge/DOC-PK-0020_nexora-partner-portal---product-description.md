# Nexora Partner Portal - Product Description

**Nexora Partner Portal** (Core Platform). Portal for channel partners.

Pricing: Flat Fee, list $600.00 per unit per month, billed monthly (product_id 12).

Key capabilities:
- Deal registration
- Lead sharing
- Partner dashboards

Limitation: Separate login domain.
