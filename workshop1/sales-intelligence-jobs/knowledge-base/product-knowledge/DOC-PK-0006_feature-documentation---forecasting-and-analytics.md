# Feature Documentation - Forecasting and Analytics

Forecast Pro builds weekly forecast snapshots from open opportunity stage probabilities and rep overrides, flags slipped deals and compares forecast to booked revenue. Revenue Analytics provides dashboards for pipeline, win rate, discount distribution, MRR/ARR movement and cohort retention. Both read from the opportunities, subscriptions and invoices objects; custom fields are available on Professional and above.
