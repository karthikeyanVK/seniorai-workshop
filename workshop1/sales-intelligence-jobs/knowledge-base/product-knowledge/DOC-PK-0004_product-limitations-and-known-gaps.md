# Product Limitations and Known Gaps

Known gaps as of 2026-11: (1) No India or APAC data residency region. (2) EU region requires the Enterprise plan; it is not available on Professional. (3) Starter has no SSO, API or audit logs. (4) Reporting: dashboards with more than 1 million rows load slowly (issue NX-4412, fix targeted for the December release, 2026-12-02). (5) Forecast Pro supports weekly, not daily, snapshots. (6) Mobile app works offline for read only. Sales must not promise dates outside this document.
