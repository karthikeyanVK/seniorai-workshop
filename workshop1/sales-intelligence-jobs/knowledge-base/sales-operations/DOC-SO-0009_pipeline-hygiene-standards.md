# Pipeline Hygiene Standards

Every open opportunity needs: next action date, decision maker, competitor (if known), and activity in the last 14 days. Opportunities flagged for no activity in 14 days appear in the manager dashboard; 30 days triggers escalation. Rep pipeline capacity: 25 open opportunities (Enterprise: 15). Reps above capacity must hand off or close out low-probability deals.
