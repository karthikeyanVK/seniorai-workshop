# Sales Target Policy

Annual targets are set per rep from territory capacity and prior-year bookings. Bookings count new business and expansion (renewals excluded). Attainment = YTD closed-won new+expansion ACV / prorated target. Mid-year target changes require VP Sales approval and a documented reason (e.g., territory reassignment). Managers' targets equal the sum of their reports.
