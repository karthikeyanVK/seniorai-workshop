# Discount Approval Workflow

Workflow: rep submits request in CRM with justification and competitor evidence -> system routes by discount % (see Discount Approval Policy) -> approver responds within 2 business days -> approval stamped on the opportunity -> order form generated. Approvals are required before the proposal is sent. Retroactive approvals are tracked as exceptions. The weekly deal desk reviews any discount above 20% and any won deal with discount above policy.
