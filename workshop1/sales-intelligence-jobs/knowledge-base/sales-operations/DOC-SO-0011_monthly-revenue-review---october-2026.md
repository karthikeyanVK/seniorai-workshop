# Monthly Revenue Review - October 2026

Closed-won deals rose from 22 in September to 44 in October 2026, but closed-won value fell from $2,026,825 to $1,532,873. Average deal size dropped because October bookings were dominated by Starter and Professional deals (only 0 Enterprise new-business wins in October versus a heavier Enterprise mix in September).
Invoiced net revenue (invoice_amount less discount_amount) fell from $3,782,233 in September to $2,115,609 in October. September included large annual invoices on 6 Enterprise accounts (CUST-0001, CUST-0010, CUST-0016, CUST-0017, CUST-0021, CUST-0026) that bill every September; October has no comparable annual billing cycle. Note that October deals have not yet reached their first invoice.
Open question for Finance: how much of the gap is billing timing versus real contraction? Check cancelled subscriptions and discounts before concluding.
