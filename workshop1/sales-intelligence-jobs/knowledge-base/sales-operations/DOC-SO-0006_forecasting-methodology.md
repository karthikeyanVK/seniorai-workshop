# Forecasting Methodology

Forecast categories: **Commit** (Negotiation, decision maker engaged, activity in last 14 days), **Best Case** (Proposal+), **Pipeline** (earlier). Weighted forecast = sum(deal_value x probability). Managers submit a commit by the 3rd business day of the quarter and update weekly. Known biases: reps over-call Proposal-stage deals and under-date slippage; apply a 15% haircut to deals whose expected_close_date has already been pushed once. Compare commit vs. actual in the quarterly forecast review.
