# Sales Commission Policy FY2026

Commission rates on first-year ACV: new business 8%, expansion 4%, renewals 1.5%. Attainment accelerator: 1.5x on bookings above 120% of target. Commission is paid on cash receipt of the first invoice; clawback applies if the customer cancels or the first invoice remains unpaid after 90 days. Targets are annual and prorated for joiners. Reps under 60% attainment for two consecutive quarters enter a performance plan.
