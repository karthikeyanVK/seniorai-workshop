# Q3 2026 Forecast vs Actual Review

Management commit for Q3 2026 new-business + expansion bookings was $4,271,000. Actual closed-won bookings (new + expansion) in Q3 were $3,095,140 (-28%). 1 opportunities with an expected close date in Q3 (value $11,400) either closed later or remain open - several were re-dated without a reason. Reps over-called Proposal-stage deals; Enterprise deals slipped most. A 15% slippage haircut would have narrowed but not closed the gap. Renewals are excluded from this commit figure.
