# Revenue Recognition Guidelines

Subscription revenue is recognised ratably over the term. monthly_recurring_revenue x 12 = annual_contract_value. Invoices are issued in advance by the product billing frequency (monthly, quarterly or annual). discount_amount on an invoice is a one-off credit; contract discounts are already reflected in deal_value. Tax is added per region (US/Canada sales tax average 7%, EU VAT 20%, India GST 18%, APAC 10%). Standard terms net 30; Enterprise plan net 45. Invoice history in the reporting database starts at the billing-system cut-over date.
