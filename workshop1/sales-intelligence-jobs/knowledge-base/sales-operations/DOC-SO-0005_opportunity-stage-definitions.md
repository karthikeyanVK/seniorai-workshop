# Opportunity Stage Definitions

Prospecting (10%): target identified, no confirmed need. Qualification (20%): need and budget confirmed. Discovery (40%): requirements and stakeholders mapped. Proposal (60%): priced proposal delivered. Negotiation (80%): commercial and legal terms under discussion. Closed Won (100%) and Closed Lost (0%). Expected close dates in the past must be re-dated with a reason. Renewal opportunities use the same stages: Discovery = health review, Proposal = renewal quote sent, Negotiation = paperwork.
