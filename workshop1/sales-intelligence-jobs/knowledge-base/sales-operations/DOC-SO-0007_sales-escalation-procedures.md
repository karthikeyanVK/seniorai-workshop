# Sales Escalation Procedures

Level 1 (rep -> regional manager): deal inactive 30 days, discount above authority, objection unresolved after two calls. Level 2 (manager -> VP Sales): deal above $150k inactive 21 days, competitor price war, executive request. Level 3 (VP -> CRO): discount above 30%, contractual exceptions. Customer-facing escalations (renewal at risk, unresolved P1/P2 product issues) are co-owned with Customer Success and Support; the account manager must contact the customer within 2 business days of escalation.
