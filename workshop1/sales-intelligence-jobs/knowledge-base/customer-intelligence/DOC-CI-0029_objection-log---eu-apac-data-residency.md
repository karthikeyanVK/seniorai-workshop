# Objection Log - EU/APAC Data Residency

Six open deals (OPP-0613, OPP-0646, OPP-0654, OPP-0535, OPP-0716, OPP-0658) cite in-region data hosting as the blocker. Enablement from early 2026 told reps 'EU hosting available' without stating it is Enterprise-only (GA 2026-09-15); some Professional-tier buyers were therefore promised hosting they cannot buy. The APAC (Sydney) region is not available until Q1 2027 and India has no region. Suggested handling: qualify residency at discovery, offer the Enterprise plan to EU buyers, and escalate APAC deals to Product.
