# Pricing Objection Handling

Common pricing objections: (1) "Competitor is 20-30% cheaper." Re-anchor on TCO and modules included in the plan. (2) "Budget is frozen." Offer quarterly billing or a phased start. (3) "We only need a few features." Sell the right plan (Starter / Professional) rather than discounting Enterprise. (4) "We want a multi-year price lock." Trade a lock for a 24-month term. Document the objection in the activity notes - pricing objections are the leading cited reason in the win/loss review.
