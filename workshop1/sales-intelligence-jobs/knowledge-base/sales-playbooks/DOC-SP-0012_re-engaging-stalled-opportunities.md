# Re-engaging Stalled Opportunities

An opportunity with no activity for 30 days is stalled. Steps: (1) check the CRM for the last customer-side response, (2) send a break-up style email offering a decision date, (3) offer a manager call, (4) if no reply in 14 days, move to Closed Lost with reason No Decision. Do not leave stalled deals in Proposal/Negotiation - they inflate the forecast.
