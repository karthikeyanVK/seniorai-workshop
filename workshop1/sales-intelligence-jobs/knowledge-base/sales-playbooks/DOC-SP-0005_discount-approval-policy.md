# Discount Approval Policy

Discount is measured against list price (discount_percentage on the opportunity).
| Discount | Approver |
|---|---|
| 0-10% | Sales rep (Starter capped at 10%) |
| >10-20% | Regional Sales Manager |
| >20-30% | VP Sales + Finance |
| >30% | Chief Revenue Officer, written exception, multi-year or strategic logo only |
Approvals must be recorded in the CRM before the order form is sent. Discounts above 30% without a recorded CRO exception are a policy breach and are reviewed in the weekly deal desk. Renewals may not be discounted more than 5% without regional manager approval.
