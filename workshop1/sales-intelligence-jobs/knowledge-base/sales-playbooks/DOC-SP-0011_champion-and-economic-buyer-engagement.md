# Champion and Economic Buyer Engagement

A champion is not an economic buyer. Record both in the CRM. If your champion goes quiet, contact the economic buyer within 5 days with a short value recap. For renewals, always know the finance owner - overdue invoices on renewed accounts are usually a purchase-order or contact-change problem, not a dispute.
