# Competitor Objection Handling

- **Vantara Revenue Cloud** - "Vantara is cheaper in year one." Counter: compare 3-year TCO including implementation (Vantara typically needs partner-led setup). Highlight native forecasting and faster time to value. Be honest that Vantara has had an EU-hosted region longer than we have.
- **PipelineIQ** - "Simpler." Counter: reporting depth, contract and renewal workflows.
- **ClearPath CRM** - price-led SMB option. Counter: Starter plan, migration help.
- **Strata Sales Suite** - strong in APAC integrations; counter with Data Connect and local partners.
- **Orbit Revenue** - AI forecasting start-up; counter with proven scale, security pack and support SLAs.
Log the competitor on every opportunity; loss reasons feed the monthly win/loss review.
