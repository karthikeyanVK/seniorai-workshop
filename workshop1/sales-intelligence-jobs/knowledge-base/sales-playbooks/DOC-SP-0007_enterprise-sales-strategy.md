# Enterprise Sales Strategy

Enterprise deals (1,000+ employees) average 120-180 days. Multi-thread from day one: at least three contacts including one decision maker. Run a technical validation (SSO, data residency, integrations) in parallel with commercial discovery. Offer a paid pilot for >$250k deals. Assign an Enterprise rep only; Mid-Market reps may not hold more than two Enterprise opportunities. An executive sponsor from Nexora leadership is engaged at Proposal stage for deals above $200k.
