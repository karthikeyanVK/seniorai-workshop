# Lead Qualification Guidelines

Qualify every inbound or outbound lead against five criteria before creating an opportunity: **Budget** (named owner and range), **Authority** (decision maker or confirmed sponsor), **Need** (a pain tied to forecasting, pipeline visibility or renewals), **Timeline** (decision inside 180 days) and **Fit** (>= 50 employees, CRM in place).
- Lead score >= 80 must be contacted within 24 hours (see Lead Assignment Rules).
- A lead with score >= 85 and no touch in 14 days must be reassigned by the regional manager.
- Disqualify with a reason code: No Budget, No Need, Competitor Locked-in, Student/Research, Duplicate.
- A high score driven only by content downloads is NOT qualification. Score reflects interest; qualification needs a two-way conversation. Treat leads with 3 unanswered attempts as nurture, not pipeline.
