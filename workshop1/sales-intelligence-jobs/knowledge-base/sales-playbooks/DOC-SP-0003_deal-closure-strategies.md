# Deal Closure Strategies

Closing is a mutual plan, not a final push. Use a **mutual close plan** with dated milestones: security review, legal redlines, procurement onboarding, signature.
1. Confirm the economic buyer and signing authority by Proposal stage.
2. Send the order form with the proposal, not after.
3. Trade, don't give: every concession (discount, extended term, free module) is exchanged for something (multi-year, reference, faster signature).
4. If a deal has no customer-side activity for 21 days, re-engage the champion and the economic buyer separately.
5. Quarter-end urgency only works if the customer has a real reason; invented deadlines raise discount requests.
