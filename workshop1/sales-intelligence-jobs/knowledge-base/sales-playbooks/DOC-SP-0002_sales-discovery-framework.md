# Sales Discovery Framework

Discovery runs in three calls. **Call 1 (Situation):** current tools, team size, forecast process, last quarter's accuracy. **Call 2 (Impact):** quantify the cost of slipped deals, stale pipeline, surprise churn. **Call 3 (Vision):** agree success criteria and who signs.
Always ask: Who else is involved? What happens if you do nothing? What have you tried? Which integrations are mandatory (CRM, ERP, billing)? Where must data be hosted?
Capture data residency, SSO and security-review requirements in the first two calls. Surfacing them late is the most common reason deals stall in Negotiation.
