# Buyer Research Summary - Why Customers Choose and Leave

Interviews with 24 buyers and 11 churned customers. Top reasons to choose Nexora: forecasting depth (62%), renewal workflows (41%), single platform (38%). Top reasons to leave or not buy: price vs. cheaper tools (46%), missing data residency (27%), low adoption after onboarding (24%), reporting speed on large datasets (14%). Churned customers rarely complained about features; most had low adoption and little contact from their account manager in the 60 days before renewal.
