# Competitive Intelligence Report 2026

## 1. Executive summary

The revenue intelligence market is consolidating. Buyers want one platform for forecasting, contracts, renewals and customer health, and they increasingly ask how AI answers are grounded and audited. Price pressure is strongest in the Mid-Market, where entry vendors undercut list prices by 20 to 35 percent. Data sovereignty is the fastest-growing deal blocker in Europe and Asia-Pacific. Nexora wins on forecasting depth, renewal workflows and a single traceable platform. It loses most often when price is the only criterion, when in-region hosting is a hard requirement outside the Enterprise EU case, or when adoption after onboarding is poor.

Five competitors matter: Vantara Revenue Cloud, PipelineIQ, ClearPath CRM, Strata Sales Suite and Orbit Revenue. Vantara is the most important because it has appeared in a rising share of European Mid-Market deals since March 2026.

## 2. Market trends

### 2.1 Platform consolidation

Point tools for forecasting, CPQ and renewals are being replaced by platforms. Buyers ask for one vendor, one data model and one security review. This favours Nexora's module approach but raises the bar on integration depth.

### 2.2 AI in sales tooling

Assistants that answer questions over pipeline data are becoming a checkbox. Buyers ask which records support an answer. Nexora's AI Revenue Copilot cites its sources, which is a differentiator against start-ups with stronger demos but weaker governance.

### 2.3 Data sovereignty

EU and APAC buyers increasingly require in-region hosting, often driven by regulators. Nexora's Frankfurt region (Enterprise only, generally available since 2026-09-15) helps Enterprise deals but not Professional buyers. APAC (Sydney) is planned for Q1 2027 and there is no India region.

### 2.4 Pricing pressure

Entry vendors price at $35 to $60 per user per month against Nexora's $95. In observed 2026 deals Vantara discounted heavily to win European Mid-Market business.

## 3. Competitor profiles

### 3.1 Vantara Revenue Cloud

**Positioning.** Enterprise-grade revenue platform, strongest in Europe.

**Strengths.** EU and APAC data regions, a large ecosystem of implementation partners and brand recognition.

**Weaknesses.** Slow implementations of 12 to 20 weeks, add-on pricing for renewal management and support quality complaints.

**Pricing.** About $110 per user per month at list. Effective prices of $78 to $85 per user per month in European Mid-Market deals in 2026, which means discounts of 25 to 30 percent.

**Where it appears.** Rising number of European Mid-Market new-business deals since March 2026.

**How we win.** Compare three-year total cost including implementation. Highlight native forecasting and faster time to value. Be candid that Vantara has hosted in the EU longer.

**How we lose.** Residency requirement surfaced after the proposal; price-led buyers; partner-led implementation preferences.

### 3.2 PipelineIQ

**Positioning.** Low-cost pipeline tool for Mid-Market.

**Strengths.** Price at $60 per user and simplicity.

**Weaknesses.** Shallow analytics, no renewal or contract workflows and limited integrations.

**Effective price.** $48 to $55 in observed deals.

**How we win.** Reporting depth and renewal workflows when finance or customer success is involved.

**How we lose.** Buyers who only want pipeline visibility.

### 3.3 ClearPath CRM

**Positioning.** SMB CRM with sales reporting.

**Strengths.** $35 per user, strong in North America and India for companies below 100 employees.

**Weaknesses.** Weak forecasting and enterprise security.

**How we win.** Starter plan with a migration offer.

**How we lose.** Very price-sensitive SMB buyers.

### 3.4 Strata Sales Suite

**Positioning.** Asia-Pacific incumbent.

**Strengths.** Local integrations, APAC data hosting and local support. $85 per user at list.

**Weaknesses.** Less depth in forecasting and global reporting.

**Effective price.** About $70 in APAC deals.

**How we win.** Forecasting depth, global reporting, Data Connect and local partners.

**How we lose.** Residency-driven deals in Australia, Singapore and Japan until the APAC region exists.

### 3.5 Orbit Revenue

**Positioning.** AI-first forecasting start-up (Series B).

**Strengths.** Strong demos and AI positioning. $120 per user.

**Weaknesses.** Limited enterprise controls and a small support team.

**How we win.** Proven scale, the Security and Compliance Pack, support SLAs and audited AI answers.

**How we lose.** Technology buyers swayed by demo impact over governance.

## 6. Regional opportunity

### 6.1 North America

The largest region with about 35 percent of customers. Demand is led by financial services and technology. Renewal management and analytics modules are the main expansion paths. Risk: Orbit and PipelineIQ pressure in Mid-Market technology accounts.

### 6.2 Europe

Strong demand in manufacturing, logistics and pharma. Buyers frequently require EU data residency and GDPR assurances. Vantara is the main competitor and is aggressive on price in Mid-Market. The Frankfurt region helps Enterprise deals only.

### 6.3 India

Fast-growing Mid-Market and SMB demand that is price-sensitive; GST of 18 percent applies on invoices. Buyers ask for local hosting and none exists. Channel partners help in IT services and BFSI. Expansion potential in Conversation Intelligence and Mobile Field Sales.

### 6.4 Asia-Pacific

Australia, Singapore, Japan and New Zealand. Residency and local language support decide many deals; Strata is the main competitor. Until the Sydney region arrives, residency-sensitive deals are at risk and should be flagged to Product.

## 7. Win and loss patterns

### 7.1 Why customers choose Nexora

Interviews with 24 buyers and 11 churned customers found the top reasons to choose Nexora: forecasting depth (62 percent), renewal workflows (41 percent) and a single platform (38 percent).

### 7.2 Why customers leave or do not buy

Price versus cheaper tools (46 percent), missing data residency (27 percent), low adoption after onboarding (24 percent) and reporting speed on large datasets (14 percent). Churned customers rarely complained about features. Most had low adoption and little contact from their account manager in the 60 days before renewal.

### 7.3 Competitor loss pattern

Lost deals with a recorded competitor are dominated by Vantara. The pattern is European Professional-tier new business since March 2026, where Vantara's effective price is about 25 to 30 percent below ours and it already hosts in the EU. Losses cluster where the residency requirement appears after the proposal. Several losses sit with the same Mid-Market rep, so competitor effect and rep effect overlap; analyse both before drawing a conclusion.

### 7.4 What to do about it

1. Qualify residency in the first two discovery calls.
2. Offer the Enterprise plan to EU buyers who need hosting, and do not promise hosting on Professional.
3. Prepare a three-year total cost comparison against Vantara.
4. Coach discovery depth for reps with a high loss count.
5. Log the competitor and loss reason on every opportunity.

## 8. Positioning statement

For revenue leaders who need one trusted view of pipeline, renewals and revenue, Nexora is the revenue intelligence platform that connects forecasting, contracts and customer health. Unlike point tools, every number is traceable to a CRM record and every AI answer cites its source. Proof points: forecast accuracy improvement, renewal visibility 120 days out and audited AI answers.

## 9. Campaign learnings

Campaign performance reviews show a common pattern: large registration numbers do not mean pipeline. Webinar registrants skew to students and small agencies with low fit, follow-up sequences that start weeks late waste intent, and leads wait several days for a rep. The fix is registration qualification, a 48-hour follow-up sequence and a score threshold that includes engagement beyond downloads. Marketing and Sales each hold part of the truth, so review lead quality and follow-up speed together.
