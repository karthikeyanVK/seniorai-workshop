# Win/Loss Analysis 2025-2026 - Competitor Patterns

Of 287 lost opportunities, competitors are recorded on 158: Vantara Revenue Cloud 48, ClearPath CRM 40, PipelineIQ 35, Strata Sales Suite 20, Orbit Revenue 15.
Vantara Revenue Cloud accounts for 48 losses and is concentrated in European Professional-tier (Mid-Market) new-business deals since March 2026 (19 in Europe). Stated reasons: lower effective price (25-30% below our list), existing EU hosting, partner-led implementation. We lose most often when the buyer's data residency requirement surfaces after Proposal. Several losses sit with the same Mid-Market rep (REP-15), so rep effect and competitor effect overlap - analyse both before concluding.
