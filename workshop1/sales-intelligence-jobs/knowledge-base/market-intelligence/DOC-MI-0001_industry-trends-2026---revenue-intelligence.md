# Industry Trends 2026 - Revenue Intelligence

Three shifts: (1) Consolidation of point tools into platforms - buyers want one vendor for forecasting, CPQ and renewals. (2) AI assistants in sales tooling - buyers ask how answers are grounded and audited. (3) Data sovereignty - EU and APAC buyers increasingly require in-region hosting. Pricing pressure is strongest in Mid-Market where entry vendors undercut by 20-35%.
