# Regional Market Opportunity - Asia-Pacific

Australia, Singapore, Japan and New Zealand. Data residency and local language support decide many deals; Strata is the main competitor. An APAC (Sydney) region is planned for Q1 2027 - until then, residency-sensitive deals are at risk and should be flagged to Product.
