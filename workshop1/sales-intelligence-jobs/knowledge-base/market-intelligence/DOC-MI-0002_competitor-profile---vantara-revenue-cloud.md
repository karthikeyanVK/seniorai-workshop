# Competitor Profile - Vantara Revenue Cloud

Enterprise-grade revenue platform, strongest in Europe. Strengths: EU/APAC data regions, ecosystem of implementation partners, brand. Weaknesses: slower implementations (12-20 weeks), add-on pricing for renewals, support quality complaints. Pricing: roughly $110/user/month; frequently discounts 25-30% to win Mid-Market in Europe in 2026. Seen in a rising number of our European Mid-Market deals since March 2026.
