# Campaign Post-mortem - Global Revenue Intelligence Webinar Series 2026

Global Revenue Intelligence Webinar Series 2026 (2026-03-02 to 2026-05-29, budget $150,000) generated 138 leads but only 3 converted to opportunities. Lead statuses: Converted 3, Contacted 88, Disqualified 47. Cost per lead about $1,087. Findings: registrants skewed to students and small agencies (low fit), no dedicated follow-up sequence ran for 3 weeks after the last session, and many leads waited several days for a rep. Marketing says lead quality was acceptable; Sales says the audience was wrong - the data supports parts of both.
