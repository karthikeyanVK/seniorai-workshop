# Competitor Profile - PipelineIQ

Low-cost pipeline tool for Mid-Market. Strengths: price ($60/user), simplicity. Weaknesses: shallow analytics, no renewal or contract workflows, limited integrations. Wins when buyers only need visibility; loses when finance or customer success is involved.
